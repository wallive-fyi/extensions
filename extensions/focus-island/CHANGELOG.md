# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Pomodoro sessions with short and long breaks, started from the launcher, the page, the island or an agent.
- A ring and a countdown on the Dynamic Island, a card with a sound at the end of each session and break, and the time left in the menu bar.
- Drift alerts for apps on an editable distraction list, with a one-minute cooldown.
- A page with today's sentence, a year heat map, a weekly report and the sessions of a picked day.
- The commands start, pause, resume, stop, skip, rest, status and stats for agents and the CLI.
- Chinese and English.
